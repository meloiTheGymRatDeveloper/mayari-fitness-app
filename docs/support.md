## Frequently Asked Questions

### Getting Started

**How do I create an account?**
Download Mayari, tap Get Started, and follow the onboarding steps. It takes less than 3 minutes to set up your profile.

**Is Mayari free?**
Yes — Mayari has a free tier that includes workout logging, food diary, barcode scanner, streaks, and personal records. Upgrade to Pro (₱89/month or ₱799/year) to unlock AI coaching, photo calorie estimation, voice food logging, advanced analytics, and more.

### Subscription & Billing

**How do I subscribe to Pro?**
Go to Profile → Subscription and choose your plan. Payment is processed securely via PayMongo (GCash, Maya, Visa, Mastercard accepted).

**How do I cancel my subscription?**
You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime through your App Store account settings: Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Mayari.

**Do you offer refunds?**
Refunds are handled by Apple in accordance with their refund policy. You can request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

**What is the referral discount?**
Share your referral code with a friend. When they subscribe, you get ₱20/month off your next billing cycle. The discount applies every month your referred friend stays active.

### Workouts

**How do I log a workout?**
Tap the Workout tab → choose your workout type (Gym, Home, Running, or Cycling) → start your session and log your sets as you go.

**How does autofill work?**
Mayari remembers your previous session's weights and reps for each exercise. When you start a new session, it pre-fills your last performance so you can focus on beating it.

**What is the rest timer?**
After logging a set, a rest timer starts automatically. You can adjust the duration in your workout settings.

---

### Nutrition

**How do I log food?**
Tap the Nutrition tab → tap the + button → choose from search, barcode scan, photo estimation, or voice logging.

**Can I log Philippine foods?**
Yes — Mayari includes a Philippine food database with local favorites. You can also search the international database or use AI food lookup for foods not found anywhere else.

**How does photo calorie estimation work?**
Take or upload a photo of your meal. Mayari's AI (powered by Anthropic Claude) analyzes the photo and estimates the calories and macronutrients. This is a Pro feature.

**How does voice food logging work?**
Tap the microphone icon and say what you ate — in Tagalog, English, or Taglish. Mayari identifies the food and logs it automatically. This is a Pro feature.

### Coach Mayari (AI)

**What is Coach Mayari?**
Coach Mayari is your AI fitness and nutrition coach, powered by Anthropic Claude. It gives you personalized workout tips, nutrition feedback, and proactive analysis based on your data.

**Does Coach Mayari speak Filipino?**
Yes — Coach Mayari responds in whatever language you write in. Write in Tagalog and it responds in Tagalog.

**Is my data used to train AI models?**
No. Your data is used only to generate your coaching responses. It is not used to train any AI models.

### Account & Data

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes all your data within 30 days.

**How do I export my data?**
Email us at meloi.m.magpantay@gmail.com with the subject "Data Export Request" and we will send you a copy of your data.

**I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
On the login screen, tap "Forgot Password" and enter your email. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.
