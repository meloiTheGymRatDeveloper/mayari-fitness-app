# Privacy Policy — Mayari

**Effective Date:** June 1, 2026
**Last Updated:** June 5, 2026

---

Mayari ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and share your information when you use the Mayari mobile application ("App").

By using Mayari, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### a. Account Information
When you register, we collect:
- Email address
- Username and display name
- Date of birth
- Password (stored as a secure hash — we never store your plain-text password)

### b. Health & Fitness Data
To provide workout and nutrition tracking, we collect:
- Body weight, height, and target weight
- Body measurements (waist, chest, arms, legs, body fat percentage)
- Workout logs: exercises, sets, reps, weights lifted
- Food logs: meals, portions, calorie and macro estimates
- Personal records and fitness goals
- Intermittent fasting schedules and logs
- Streak data (workout and nutrition consistency)

### c. Photos
If you use the **Photo Calorie Estimation** feature, you may choose to upload a photo of your food. This photo is:
- Uploaded to secure cloud storage (Supabase)
- Analyzed by an AI model (Anthropic Claude) to estimate calorie and macro content
- Stored temporarily for processing; you may delete your photos at any time

We do not use your food photos for any purpose other than generating calorie estimates for you.

### d. Payment Information
When you subscribe to Mayari Pro, payments are processed by **PayMongo** (our third-party payment processor). We do not store your full card number, CVV, or GCash/Maya credentials on our servers. We receive and store:
- Subscription status and tier
- Amount paid and billing period
- PayMongo customer and subscription IDs (for managing renewals)

### e. Voice Data
If you use **Voice Food Logging**, your voice recording is:
- Transcribed locally or via the device's speech recognition
- The resulting text transcript is sent to our AI service for food identification
- The audio recording itself is not stored on our servers

### f. Communications with Coach Mayari
Messages you send to Coach Mayari (AI coaching tips and analysis) are:
- Processed by Anthropic Claude AI to generate personalized fitness guidance
- Stored in our database so you can view your coaching history
- Used only to provide coaching responses — not used to train AI models

### g. Device & Usage Data
We automatically collect limited technical information including:
- Device type and operating system version
- App version
- Push notification token (for sending you alerts and reminders)
- Crash logs and error reports (for fixing bugs)

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

- **Provide the App** — track workouts, log food, generate AI coaching tips, and monitor your progress
- **Personalize your experience** — tailor workout plans, calorie goals, and coaching based on your profile
- **Send push notifications** — streak reminders, weekly summaries, PR alerts, subscription updates (you can disable these in Settings)
- **Process payments** — manage your subscription and apply referral discounts
- **Generate BIR-compliant receipts** — if required, we use JuanTax to issue official invoices to your registered email after a successful payment
- **Improve the App** — analyze usage patterns and fix issues (using anonymized, aggregated data only)
- **Customer support** — respond to your questions and reports

---

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only as follows:

### Third-Party Service Providers
We use the following trusted service providers to operate the App:

| Provider | Purpose | Data Shared |
|---|---|---|
| **Supabase** (USA) | Database, authentication, file storage | All app data stored securely |
| **Anthropic** (USA) | AI coaching, photo calorie estimation, voice food logging | Food photos, coaching messages, food text |

| **JuanTax / Juan** (Philippines) | BIR-compliant invoice generation | Name, email, amount paid |

All providers are bound by data processing agreements and are prohibited from using your data for their own purposes.

### Legal Requirements
We may disclose your information if required by Philippine law, court order, or government authority, or if necessary to protect the rights, safety, or property of Mayari or its users.

---

## 4. Data Storage & Security

- All data is stored on **Supabase** servers with row-level security (RLS) — each user can only access their own data
- Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/HTTPS) and at rest
- Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms
- Payment data is handled entirely by PayMongo and never touches our servers in raw form
- We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, all your personal data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

---

## 5. Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your data — view everything we store about you in the App
- **Correct** your data — update your profile, goals, and measurements at any time
- **Delete** your data — delete your account from Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes all your data.
- **Export** your data — contact us to request a copy of your data
- **Opt out of notifications** — disable push notifications in Settings or your device settings
To exercise any of these rights, contact us at the email below.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Mayari is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it immediately.

## 7. AI Features Disclosure

Mayari uses artificial intelligence provided by **Anthropic** (maker of Claude AI) for the following features:

- **Coach Mayari** — generates personalized fitness tips and analysis based on your workout and nutrition data
- **Photo Calorie Estimation** — analyzes food photos to estimate calories and macronutrients
- **Voice Food Logging** — identifies food items from voice transcripts
- **AI Food Lookup** — estimates nutrition for foods not found in our database

Your data sent to Anthropic is used only to generate responses for you and is subject to [Anthropic's Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy). Anthropic does not use your data to train their AI models under our agreement.
